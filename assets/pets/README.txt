Pets
A free 3D model kit from Shuriken Studio
https://shuriken.studio/kits/pets

A free low-poly pet kit for Roblox Studio: 55 matching GLB models for a pet-collecting game, an egg-hatching simulator or a pet shop roleplay. Everyday pets include an orange tabby kitten, a husky puppy and a chunky bunny, and ocean friends such as a baby whale and a jumping dolphin. Jungle cubs like a tiger cub, a lion cub and a hanging sloth sit beside rarer finds, with a rainbow unicorn, a baby dragon, a phoenix chick and a griffin chick at the top of the rarity ladder. Odd ones like a gummy bear pet and a rock golem keep the roster surprising. An egg hatching stand is included as the centrepiece of a hatching area. With this many pets, you can fill common, rare and mythical tiers without hunting for extra models, and still have spares for limited events. All 55 models are free to use in your own games, and because they share one chunky style, a new pet never looks out of place.

Using them
  Roblox Studio: Import 3D, choose a GLB, insert.
  Blender: File > Import > glTF 2.0.
  Unity, Godot, Unreal, three.js: import directly or through glTF.
